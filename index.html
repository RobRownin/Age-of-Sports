Beyond the arena

Missions

Missions

While your athlete is away, your own training supports them – only sessions started after the mission began and finished before it ends count. Reaching the mission's training goal raises the success chance by up to 25 points, halves the injury risk and adds Kleos (only you see your progress). Away athletes can't fight. Rarely a mission ends gloriously – or with an injury.
